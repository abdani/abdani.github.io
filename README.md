# abdani.github.io

Source of [abdani.github.io](https://abdani.github.io/) — the home page that lists the apps by Dani Abdani:

- [Donat TeleDrive](https://abdani.github.io/Donat-TeleDrive/) — Your own private cloud drive for Windows, powered by your Telegram account. Verified transfers, recovery-first sync, 11 themes.
- [Donat Downloader](https://abdani.github.io/Donat-Downloader/) — A fast, modern downloader for video and music from YouTube and 1,700+ sites. Copy a link, pick a format, done.
- [Donat Screen](https://abdani.github.io/Donat-Screen/) — A tiny, portable screenshot tool that lives in the tray. One hotkey, clipboard first, saved where you want it.
- [Android Media Separator](https://abdani.github.io/Android-Media-Separator/) — Sorts an Android phone's photos, videos, audio and documents into neat folders on your PC — over USB or from a folder.
